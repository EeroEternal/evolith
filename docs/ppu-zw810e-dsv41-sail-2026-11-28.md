# Multi-Generation Evolution on 8 × T-Head PPU-ZW810E (`SAIL 1.7`, DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash)

## Scope & Measurement Boundaries

`zstep-sail-evolve` (`src/evolith/lab/zstep_sail.py`, `src/evolith/lab/zstep_sail_bench.py`) implements an end-to-end measurement-driven optimization loop for `DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash` (`476 GiB`, `TP=8`) on `8 × T-Head Zhenwu 810E` (`PPU-ZW810E`, `SAIL 1.7` / `HGGC 12.3` / `triton-for-sail 3.4`):

1. **Real Hardware Operator Observation**: Measures per-family CUDA-event device latency shares across all 40 layers under representative `TP=8` continuous-batching workloads (`mxfp4_moe_prefill`, `mxfp4_moe_decode`, `sparse_attn_tp8`, `mhc_mixes`, `icn_allreduce`, `attn_qkv_o_proj`, `engram_compressor_indexer`, `lm_head`).
2. **Independent Micro-Benchmark Confirmation**: Evaluates candidate policies in isolated processes across observed batch shapes against independent FP32/FP64 references, requiring fresh-seed confirmation (`seed=31` then `seed=73`).
3. **Alternating AB/BA Full-Model Gate**: Compares each proposed policy against the current approved incumbent over `5` alternating AB/BA `TP=8` trials (`decide(..., min_gain=0.03)` on 99% one-sided bootstrap CI lower bound, `CV <= 0.10`, and `100%` greedy token parity on `prime_squares_table`).
4. **Explicit Capability Boundaries & Archive Resilience**: Unsupported operator bottlenecks in earlier generations were recorded in `capability_gaps` (closed to `[]` in Generation 3); Generation 4 (`SailPolicy v4`) adds Ozaki `2×BF16->FP32` Tensor-Core projections, warp-`__shfl_sync` FP4 register-LUT MoE GEMV, single-pass vectorized Split-K `mHC` + 16-lane warp Sinkhorn, pipelined Flash-Decoding Split-K sparse MQA, and same-context archive recovery across transient SSH transport drops.
