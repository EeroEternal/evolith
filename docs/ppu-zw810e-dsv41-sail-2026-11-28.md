# Multi-Generation Evolution on 8 × T-Head PPU-ZW810E (`SAIL 1.7`, DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash)

## Scope & Measurement Boundaries

`zstep-sail-evolve` (`src/evolith/lab/zstep_sail.py`, `src/evolith/lab/zstep_sail_bench.py`) implements an end-to-end measurement-driven optimization loop for `DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash` (`476 GiB`, `TP=8`) on `8 × T-Head Zhenwu 810E` (`PPU-ZW810E`, `SAIL 1.7` / `HGGC 12.3` / `triton-for-sail 3.4`):

1. **Real Hardware Operator Observation**: Measures per-family CUDA-event device latency shares across all 40 layers under representative `TP=8` continuous-batching workloads (`moe_experts`, `sparse_attn`, `mhc_mixes`, `icn_allreduce`, `attn_qkv_o_proj`, `engram_compressor_indexer`).
2. **Independent Micro-Benchmark Confirmation**: Evaluates candidate policies in isolated processes across observed batch shapes, requiring fresh-seed confirmation (`seed + 73`) and non-regression across shapes (`min_shape_ratio >= 0.90`).
3. **Alternating AB/BA Full-Model Gate**: Compares each proposed policy against the current approved incumbent over `5` alternating AB/BA `TP=8` trials (`decide(..., min_gain=0.03)` on 99% bootstrap CI lower bound, `100%` greedy token parity).
4. **Explicit Capability Boundaries**: Unsupported operator bottlenecks (`attn_qkv_o_proj`, `engram_compressor_indexer`) are recorded in `capability_gaps` rather than masked or skipped silently.

## Explicit Remaining Capability Gaps

In `20261127T212159Z-94049bc620a1` (`docs/benchmarks/ppu_zw810e_sail_20261128/gen2_approved_report.json`), after approving `moe_experts`, `sparse_attn`, `icn_allreduce`, and `mhc_mixes`, the observer recorded two remaining unsupported families as explicit `capability_gaps`:
- `attn_qkv_o_proj` (`28.25%` share): Dense MXFP4 / BF16 QKV and O projections dispatched via `torch.matmul` / `mxfp4_linear` without custom fused projection evolution.
- `engram_compressor_indexer` (`20.43%` share): Lightning Indexer `wq_b` / `wk` projections and top-k selection not yet parameterized in `SailPolicy`.
